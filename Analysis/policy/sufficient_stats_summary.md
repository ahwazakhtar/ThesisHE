**(a)(ii) The drought-specific unpriced floor (arithmetic shown):**

- Certified premium equivalence bound (Row 8, drought L2 primary): delta* = **$7.40 per member-month (PMPM)**, verdict STRONG.
- Full-morbidity benchmark (Row 10 heat cost expressed PMPM): **$9.33-$14.75 PMPM** (= $112-$177/beneficiary-year / 12).
- delta* rules out drought pass-through **> 50.2%-79.3%** of the morbidity benchmark (= delta*/benchmark = 0.502-0.793).
- Its complement is the floor that **cannot** be priced: **>= 20.7%-49.8%** of a morbidity-scale cost (= 1 - delta*/benchmark).
- In dollars: **>= $1.93-$7.35 PMPM** (>= $23-$88 per member-year) is provably unpriced in local drought premiums.

Discipline: **drought only.** Heat/cold premium bounds are softer (delta* exceeds the benchmark; equivalence with full pass-through is not rejected), so the strong unpriced claim is not licensed for them. Premium coefficients are **never** multiplied by enrollment.

## (d) RMA framing statistic

- 2012-event local income loss (|band A1|): **$7,091,469,872**.
- RMA **drought** crop-insurance indemnity to the same 139 counties, 2012-2023: **$1,916,902,563**.
- RMA **all-peril** indemnity to those counties, 2012-2023: **$5,479,927,947**.
- **Ratio: the health-finance income loss is 3.7x the federal drought-crop transfer (and 1.29x all-peril transfers) to the same counties over 2012-2023; 10.4x the 2012 drought-year crop payout alone ($680,781,354).**
- UNIT CAVEAT: the income loss is an event-level ITT (per-capita x population, one event); RMA is a multi-year dollar flow. Report as a framing contrast, not a like-for-like net.

## Estimand-label ledger (binding)

- 2012 income & DRDID: **event-specific ITT of first drought onset**; do NOT annualize or generalize.
- Typical-recurring: **bounded null** (frontier CS-dr, e=0); a typical drought year's income effect is bounded near zero.
- Cold cumulative: **within-county exposure-history contrast (B6)**, estimator-dependent; NOT a marginal causal effect of assigned shock-years.
- Debt scar: **onset/exit asymmetry**; measurement-fragile (Row 24); a lower bound on people (out-migration + credit-visibility selection).
- Medicare: **administrative burden for the 65+/disabled population**, parallel direct evidence, not mediation.
- Unpriced floor: **drought-only**; a bounding share, never enrollment-scaled.
- Concentration & RMA: **descriptive / framing**, not causal welfare weights.
