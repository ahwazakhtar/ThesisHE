I have some technical questions about how you classify shocks, particularly since the long-run climate perspective should really treat shocks as a draw from a distribution of weather events, which begs the question of whether these are shocks or unantipatable events given historical averages.  It also raises questions about how you handle multiple shocks.  Much of that is definitional so we can set aside from now.  I find the biggest question here is on mechanisms.  I can easily imagine cold shocks and drought have profound impacts on agriculture, which then have profound impacts on income.  I believe there are studies on this in developing country contexts.  How much of what you’re finding cannot be explained by that channel?  And what other channels might be in play?  I think the key to success here is not just showing the reduced form relationship but pointing readers to the mechanisms that might underlie them even if you cannot perfectly identify those.  Does that makes sense?
