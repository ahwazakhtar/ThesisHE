Feedback from Committee April 2026

Here is the feedback from the thesis committee in April 2026.

Econometric: 
1. check random effects
2. check lag tests. For example, check if a county was in a shock one year and then it exits, what effect does it have on the spending?
3. we are looking for some smaller scale "natural experiments" so we can do diff in diffs. Are there never exposed counties?

General
1. proposed propagation pathways should be backed up with evidence. For example, heat leading to delayed care should be substantiated. Similarly, changing care utilization patterns with cold should be backed up by data.

Environmental
1. we need to take into account humidity. There is data here https://prism.oregonstate.edu/documents/PRISM_downloads_web_service.pdf and we are interested in tdmean variable. This needs to be at the state level
