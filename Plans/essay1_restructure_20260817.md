## Why

The 2026-08-17 baseline decomposition (`Analysis/did/did_2x2_baseline_sensitivity_drought2012.csv`,
`did_farm_nonfarm_eventstudy_drought2012.csv`) showed the 2012 drought 2×2 income ATT of
−$1,311 is dominated by farm income reverting from the record 2011 commodity-price peak;
the baseline-invariant nonfarm component is −$261 to −$414 (≈0.5–0.8% of mean PCPI) and
never conventionally significant. The natural experiment is no longer a headline result.
The robust spine of the essay is medical: Medicare morbidity (direct, reproduces
Deryugina et al. 2019 in-panel, survives multiple-testing), the ACA pass-through null
(bounded; institutional contribution), and the debt associations (real, measurement-
fragile). The essay's primary question — *what healthcare costs follow climate shocks,
and which institutions record or price them?* — is answered by a Medicare-led paper.

## What the drought experiment still does (and where it goes)

1. It is the committee's requested natural experiment → it stays in the dissertation,
   in **Appendix A**, resolved honestly by the decomposition ("what event contrasts in
   agricultural counties measure") rather than silently dropped.
2. Income is the financing bridge (costs become *debt* when budgets cannot absorb them)
   and the incidence foundation for Essays 2–3 → a compact main-text section
   (**§6 Household economic capacity**) keeps the window-stable distributed-lag income
   result and the employment caveat, so Essays 2–3 need no restructuring.
