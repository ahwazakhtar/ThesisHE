# The Evolution of the Results: From Naive Climate Exposure to a Four-Channel Story

**Purpose:** A self-understanding document for the author — the honest, chronological account of how the analysis started (a simple climate-shock → medical-debt panel regression) and arrived at today's nuanced story. Every claim is traceable to a file or commit.
**Compiled:** 2026-07-06, from `changelog.md`, `conductor/tracks/*/spec.md|plan.md`, `Analysis/*` syntheses, and the git log.

---

## The story in one paragraph

The project began as a two-way fixed-effects panel asking whether climate shocks raise health-finance costs, and found that drought and cold raise medical debt. Three sessions of forensic pipeline repair (NOAA sentinel bug, a 12× temperature-sum error, look-ahead z-scores) proved the correlations real — and, almost as a byproduct, brought in the income and employment outcomes that became the thesis spine. Committee pressure forced a causal turn: the 2012 drought natural experiment delivered the cleanest identification (drought → income −$1,311), and the persistence and inequality tracks gave each hazard its own signature (drought *scars*, cold *compounds*, heat *saturates*; harm amplifies in high-SVI counties). Then two rounds of external hardening killed the weak links — the employment effect broke under doubly-robust and pooled Callaway–Sant'Anna estimators, medical debt was exposed as measurement-fragile, premium pass-through proved incoherent, and one celebrated heterogeneity result died in logs — while an external reader's "isn't this just agriculture?" challenge produced the final reframing: the effects run through a non-agricultural **Medicare morbidity channel** and **broad labor exposure**, with **energy burden** as a distinct distributional axis and agriculture as one event-specific channel among several. What survives is smaller than what was claimed along the way, but every surviving claim has now passed a gauntlet.

## The arc in prose

**The naive beginning.** The project opened with a straightforward two-way fixed-effects state panel (`Code/run_analysis.R`, originally `plm` + `sandwich`): PDSI binned into drought flags, state-specific temperature z-scores for heat/cold shocks, CDD quintiles, distributed 0–2-year lags, state-clustered SEs. The first results (`Analysis/state/synthesis.md` §4) were clean and intuitive — **extreme drought (lag 2) and cold shock (lag 1) both raise medical debt**, premiums respond marginally, and systemic per-capita spending just tracks income and unemployment. The county expansion added AQI (`Analysis/county/synthesis.md`, Feb 10) and found a lagged AQI→medical-debt signal, but also the first warning signs: "shock vs burden" specifications disagreed, and rating-area premium models threw spurious, singular-fit coefficients.

**The plumbing years.** Before any of this could be trusted, Sessions 1–3 (changelog 2026-02-19 through 03-03; commits `d99c564`→`670b3dc`) were a forensic pipeline rebuild. The finds were sobering: NOAA's blanket `-9.9` missing-value threshold was silently deleting legitimate Alaska/Montana cold temperatures and extreme-drought PDSI values; state temperature was being *summed* over 12 months (a 12× error); the temperature z-scores used the full-sample mean (look-ahead bias), corrected to a frozen **1990–2000 baseline** (`process_county_climate.R`); the AQI z-score "shock" was dropped as unreliable in favor of the hard EPA threshold `High_AQI_Max`; the debt-reporting exclusion had been over-broad (blanket CO/MN/NY → **CO-2023 only**, recovering ~2,600 observations). Crucially, the county socioeconomic pipeline (BEA PCPI, ACS income and employment) was added here — the **real-economy outcomes that would later become the thesis spine** entered the data almost as a byproduct of fixing the plumbing. The headline debt correlations survived the rebuild, which is what licensed everything after.

**From correlation toward cause.** The dynamic-panel work (`run_event_study.R`) was explicitly renamed from "event study" to "impulse response to *recurring* shocks" — the first formal recognition that treatment turns on and off, which would later become the ITT caveat. The April committee then demanded identification. The random-effects Hausman check rejected RE but left the headlines standing (`Analysis/robustness/synthesis.md`). The decisive move was the **2012 drought never-exposed-control DiD** (`Analysis/did/did_results.md`): 139 first-onset treated counties vs. 2,534 never-exposed, giving PCPI −$1,311 and employment −2,053. This became, in the summary's own words, "the cleanest single piece of identification in the thesis," and it relocated the mechanism from debt-correlation to a **drought → household income → later debt** channel. Humidity (PRISM `tdmean`) was added as a confounder check; the cold-debt coefficient was stable to three significant figures (`Analysis/state/humidity_sensitivity.csv`).

**Persistence and inequality — the essays take shape.** The persistence track decomposed shocks into onset/persist/exit and found that **drought debt *scars*** (h=2 onset/exit asymmetry +0.0182, p=0.0015) while **cold employment *compounds*** with cumulative dose (−5,668 by year 10) and **heat *saturates***. Demographic mediators produced a clean null (effects survive population turnover, aging, tenure). The exposure-index track interacted every shock with the CDC SVI and found **vulnerability amplifies harm on the real-economy outcomes** (heat→jobs, cold→income ~8×, drought→premiums) — but the medical-debt interaction *reversed*, which was correctly diagnosed not as counter-evidence but as a **credit-bureau measurement artifact** (poorer/uninsured areas accrue less *measured* debt). Cross-level symmetry confirmed the pattern: nulls and income/spending EJ replicate across state and county; **only medical debt is aggregation-sensitive**. This is where "lead with income/employment/premiums, treat debt as fragile" became doctrine.

**The hardening that killed things.** Two external pressures then stress-tested the surviving claims. First, the **DiD frontier track** (R 4.5.3) subjected the 2012 experiment to DRDID, Callaway–Sant'Anna, and HonestDiD. The **income effect strengthened** under covariate conditioning (−$1,451) and later cleared wild-cluster bootstrap (p=0.036) and flat 1990–2011 BEA pre-trends. The **employment effect broke**: DRDID attenuated it ~58% (−2,053 → −871), and the pooled cross-cohort CS estimator *reversed* it to +2,609 with positive pre-trends — the 2012 employment result is event-specific, not the effect of a typical drought cohort. Along the way, the "**2012 Midwest drought**" label was exposed as a **misnomer** (the extreme-PDSI cohort is Georgia + Mountain West + Plains), and the estimand was recognized as ITT because treatment is "on" only ~13% of treated post-period county-years.

Second, an **external reader challenge** (Josh Graff Zivin) — *how much of this is just agriculture?* — produced the mechanism verdict (`Analysis/mechanism/mechanism_verdict.md`): **agriculture is one channel, not the channel.** The single cleanest result is a non-agricultural **Medicare morbidity/utilization channel** (heat/cold/AQI raise standardized spending and ED visits, reproducing Deryugina et al. 2019 in-panel). **Labor exposure** is non-agricultural too (cold→employment survives in the *least*-farming counties; heat→employment loads on climate-exposed *non-farm* share). **Energy burden** is a distinct distributional axis (only r=0.11 with SVI). Agriculture survives as an event-specific drought signature partly driven by out-migration selection. The hospital track supplied the supply-side twin: heat strain concentrates on **safety-net hospitals**, mirroring the demand-side SVI amplification.

**The ongoing tightening.** The final push (July 2026) is still trimming. The premium pass-through analysis found **no coherent pass-through** — coefficients flip sign across levels of analysis — explicitly *retracting* an earlier "only heat passes through" reading as a county-spec artifact (`Analysis/mediation/premium_mediation_summary.md`). And the second-reviewer revision, re-running employment in logs, found the celebrated **−2,011 "cold effect strengthens in low-ag counties" was a county-size artifact in levels — it dies in logs** (commit `5c615dd`), while energy burden survived an interaction horse-race (`288620e`).

## What the journey teaches (meta-lessons)

1. **Plumbing precedes inference.** The three most consequential early "findings" changes came from data bugs (sentinel values, sum-vs-mean, look-ahead z-scores), not econometrics. The results that survived the rebuild earned their credibility there.
2. **Every hardening round killed something — and the story got *better* each time.** Employment's fragility forced income to the front, where the evidence is genuinely strongest. The agriculture challenge produced the Medicare morbidity channel, now the cleanest result in the thesis.
3. **Measurement fragility is a finding, not a nuisance.** The medical-debt reversal under SVI interaction is itself publishable insight about credit-bureau outcomes in poor places.
4. **Labels matter.** "Event study" → "impulse response to recurring shocks"; "Midwest drought" → GA/Mountain West/Plains; "the effect of drought" → "ITT of first onset." Each renaming was a substantive correction.
5. **The thesis spine was an accident.** Income and employment entered as controls-adjacent additions during pipeline repair and ended up carrying the causal story, while the original outcome (medical debt) became the fragile one.
