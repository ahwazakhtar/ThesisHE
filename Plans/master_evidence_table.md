Tier vocabulary: **headline** / **confirmatory** / **mechanism-supporting** / **exploratory**,
plus **RETIRED** (claims that died — kept as rows so the 1.2 rewrite knows what to remove) and a
few **framing/scope** rows that are not estimands.

**Pre-designated headline tests (audit priority 3 — lock a SMALL set; freeze the specification
search around these five):**

| # | Headline test | Row |
|---|---|---|
| H1 | 2012 drought → per-capita income loss (natural experiment) | Row 1 |
| H2 | Medicare morbidity / utilization channel (non-agricultural) | Row 10 |
| H3 | Cold employment compounds with cumulative dose | Row 17 |
| H4 | ACA premium institutional null — no coherent pass-through (**pending its MDE bound**) | Row 8 |

Everything else is confirmatory robustness, mechanism-supporting evidence, exploratory pattern,
RETIRED, or framing. Medical debt, ACA premiums (as a priced margin), and hospital-accounting
levels are demoted from headline outcomes to secondary/measurement ledgers (audit §7, §9;
clarifying-question 4 — confirmed at the 1.3 user gate).

Shared panel facts used below: **county panel** = 2011–2023, county-year, ~3,100 counties,
`fixest::feols` county+year FE, state-clustered. **State panel** = 1996–2025 (debt/premium
subsets shorter), state-year. **Medicare** = CMS Geographic Variation, 65+/disabled, 2014–2023.
**ACA premiums** = individual market, 2014–2025, rating-area / state. **Hospital** = NASHP HCT,
5,119 hospitals, 2011–2023, CCN-year (shock at hospital's location county). **2012 DiD** = 139
first-onset treated counties vs 2,534 never-exposed, ITT of first onset.
